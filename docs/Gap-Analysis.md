# Gap Analysis



## Project



BeautyBook Appointment Booking System



## Purpose



The purpose of this Gap Analysis is to compare the current appointment booking process with the proposed future process and identify areas for improvement.



---



# Executive Summary



The makeup business currently relies on WhatsApp for appointment scheduling. This process is manual, time-consuming, and prone to errors.



The proposed BeautyBook application introduces an automated booking system that improves efficiency, customer experience, and business reporting.



---



# Current State vs Future State



## Gap 1: Booking Method



### Current State (AS-IS)



Clients send WhatsApp messages to request appointments.



### Future State (TO-BE)



Clients book appointments directly through the BeautyBook application.



### Gap



No self-service booking capability currently exists.



### Benefit



Faster booking process and improved customer experience.



---



## Gap 2: Appointment Availability



### Current State (AS-IS)



The makeup artist manually checks appointment availability.



### Future State (TO-BE)



The system automatically validates available appointment slots.



### Gap



Availability checking is currently manual.



### Benefit



Reduced workload and prevention of booking conflicts.



---



## Gap 3: Booking Confirmation



### Current State (AS-IS)



Appointment confirmations are sent manually through WhatsApp.



### Future State (TO-BE)



Confirmations are automatically generated and sent to clients.



### Gap



No automated communication exists.



### Benefit



Improved efficiency and response times.



---



## Gap 4: Appointment Records



### Current State (AS-IS)



Booking information is stored within WhatsApp conversations.



### Future State (TO-BE)



All bookings are stored in a centralized database.



### Gap



No structured appointment management system exists.



### Benefit



Improved record-keeping and easier appointment management.



---



## Gap 5: Reporting and Analytics



### Current State (AS-IS)



The business cannot easily track:



- Number of bookings

- Monthly revenue

- Most popular services

- Cancellation rates



### Future State (TO-BE)



The system provides reporting dashboards and business insights.



### Gap



No reporting capability currently exists.



### Benefit



Better business decision-making.



---



# Gap Analysis Matrix



| Business Area | Current State | Future State | Benefit |

|---------------|---------------|--------------|----------|

| Booking Process | WhatsApp messages | Online booking system | Faster bookings |

| Availability Checks | Manual | Automated | Reduced errors |

| Confirmations | Manual | Automated | Better customer experience |

| Record Keeping | WhatsApp chats | Database | Improved organization |

| Reporting | Not available | Dashboards and reports | Business insights |



---



# Risks if No Changes Are Made



- Increased administrative workload

- Higher risk of double bookings

- Poor customer experience

- Difficulty scaling the business

- Limited business visibility



---



# Recommendations



1. Implement the BeautyBook booking application.

2. Automate appointment scheduling.

3. Store booking data in a centralized database.

4. Introduce booking reports and dashboards.

5. Implement automated booking confirmations.



---



# Conclusion



The analysis shows significant opportunities to improve the current appointment booking process.



The proposed BeautyBook solution will eliminate several manual activities, improve customer satisfaction, reduce scheduling conflicts, and provide valuable business insights to support future business growth.
