# Future State Analysis

## Proposed Solution

BeautyBook will provide an online booking platform allowing clients to make appointments without sending WhatsApp messages.

## Benefits

- Automated scheduling
- Reduced administrative workload
- Faster booking process
- Improved customer experience
- Elimination of double-bookings
- Centralized appointment records

## Expected Business Outcomes

- Increased efficiency
- Improved client satisfaction
- Better reporting and analytics
- Increased booking capacity
