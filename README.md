# BeautyBook Appointment Booking System

## Project Overview

BeautyBook is a Business Analyst portfolio project developed to solve appointment scheduling challenges experienced by a makeup artist who currently manages bookings through WhatsApp.

The project demonstrates business analysis techniques including requirements elicitation, process mapping, stakeholder analysis, Agile planning, SQL analysis, and dashboard reporting.

---

## Business Problem

The makeup artist currently manages appointments manually through WhatsApp.

Challenges include:

- Delayed responses
- Double bookings
- Lost messages
- Manual scheduling
- No reporting or analytics

---

## Proposed Solution

A digital appointment booking platform that enables clients to:

- View services
- Select available dates and times
- Book appointments
- Receive confirmations

The business owner can:

- Manage appointments
- View schedules
- Generate reports
- Track business performance
